The 2026-2027 Season

The season opens at Sussex County Maker Fest on October 3rd and 4th. The rest of the schedule is still being set, and a September event may be added before then.

Rules, build recommendations, and the rest of the season dates are all being decided right now, and we want them shaped by the people who build and compete. Have a proposal? A rule that needs clarifying, a safety procedure worth tightening, a build guideline that would help new competitors get in the arena? Send it our way.

Thank You for an Amazing Season

That's a wrap on 2025-2026. Eight events, hundreds of matches, and a whole lot of broken parts later, the season closed out at the New Jersey State Fair on August 1st.

Thank you to every builder who put a bot in the box, every volunteer who ran the floor and kept the stream going, every venue that hosted us, and every sponsor who made it possible. None of this happens without you.

About GSCRL

We're a not-for-profit organization that puts on combat robotics events for folks local and traveling to build and fight their best bots! Events are held live during our competition season, and streamed online. Competitors give it their all to go home with trophies, prizes, and bragging rights, and you make it all possible.

Want to help? Let us know and reach out!
